# Leave Policy

This policy applies to all permanent employees of Northwind Components Pvt. Ltd. (a fictional company used for demonstration). It replaces the leave policy dated 1 April 2023.

## Annual leave

Permanent employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave accrues monthly at 1.5 days per completed month of service. Employees who join mid-year receive leave on a pro-rata basis.

Up to 10 days of unused annual leave can be carried forward to the next calendar year. Any balance above 10 days lapses on 31 December and is not paid out.

Annual leave of three days or more must be applied for at least 7 days in advance through the HR portal. The reporting manager approves or rejects the request within 2 working days.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 8 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave cannot be carried forward. A medical certificate is required for sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days.

## Casual leave

Employees receive 6 days of casual leave per calendar year for personal matters. Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave and cannot exceed 3 consecutive days.

## Parental leave

Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave for the first two children, as per the Maternity Benefit Act. Paternity leave is 10 working days of paid leave, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption.

## Leave encashment

On resignation, employees can encash up to 15 days of accumulated annual leave at their basic salary rate. Sick leave and casual leave are not encashable.
