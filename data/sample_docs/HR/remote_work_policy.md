# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Eligibility

Employees in corporate functions (Finance, HR, IT, Sales Operations) may work remotely up to 2 days per week after completing their 3-month probation period. Plant, warehouse and quality roles are on-site roles and are not eligible for remote work.

## Core hours

Remote employees must be available online between 10:00 and 16:00 IST. Team meetings are scheduled inside core hours.

## Requesting remote days

Remote days are planned weekly and recorded in the HR portal by Friday for the following week. Managers may cancel a remote day with 24 hours notice when on-site presence is needed.

## Equipment and security

The company provides a laptop and a headset. Employees must connect through the company VPN when working remotely. Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the VPN switched on.

## Internet allowance

Employees working remotely at least 4 days a month receive an internet allowance of Rs 1,000 per month, paid with salary.
