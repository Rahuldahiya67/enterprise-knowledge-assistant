# Dispatch Standard Operating Procedure

## Dispatch planning

The dispatch plan for the next day is frozen at 17:30 every working day. Orders added after the freeze are treated as urgent dispatches and need approval from the plant head.

Before an order is planned, the dispatch team confirms two things: that full stock is available in the finished-goods store, and that the customer's payment status is cleared by the accounts team.

## Vehicle arrangement

Vehicles are arranged through transporter bidding. The dispatch team sends trip details (material, quantity, route and vehicle size) to approved transporters and selects the lowest technically suitable quote. Bids are open for 4 hours.

## Documents

Every dispatch needs a tax invoice in three copies (store, dispatch record and driver), a lorry receipt (LR) from the transporter and an e-way bill for consignments above Rs 50,000. The transporter shares the LR and e-way bill within 2 hours of loading.

## Hold reasons

When a planned order is not dispatched, the dispatch team records one hold reason: stock shortage, payment pending, vehicle not available, or customer request. Hold reasons are reported in the next morning's actual dispatch summary.

## Proof of delivery

The proof of delivery (POD) is uploaded within 3 days of delivery. Freight bills are paid only against an uploaded POD.
