# Travel and Expense Policy

## Booking travel

All business travel is booked through the travel desk at least 5 days before departure. Domestic flights are booked in economy class. Employees at grade M3 and above may book business class for flights longer than 6 hours.

Train travel in AC 2-tier is allowed for journeys under 500 km.

## Daily allowance

The daily allowance covers meals and local transport. It is Rs 2,500 per day in metro cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad) and Rs 1,800 per day in other cities.

## Hotel limits

Hotel stays are capped at Rs 6,000 per night in metro cities and Rs 4,000 per night elsewhere, excluding taxes. Stays above the cap need written approval from the department head.

## Submitting claims

Expense claims are submitted in the expense portal within 15 days of returning from the trip, with scanned original receipts for every item above Rs 500. Claims submitted after 30 days are not reimbursed.

Approved claims are reimbursed in the next salary cycle. The finance team audits 10% of claims at random every month.

## Non-reimbursable items

Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines and upgrades not covered by this policy are not reimbursed.
