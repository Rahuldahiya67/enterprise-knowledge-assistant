# Procurement Standard Operating Procedure

## Purchase requisition

Every purchase starts with a purchase requisition (PR) raised in SAP by the requesting department. The PR must name the cost centre, the item, the quantity and the required delivery date.

## Approval limits

Purchase requisitions are approved based on value:

- Up to Rs 50,000: approved by the department head.
- Rs 50,001 to Rs 5,00,000: approved by the department head and the finance controller.
- Above Rs 5,00,000: approved by the department head, the finance controller and the CFO.

## Quotations

Purchases above Rs 1,00,000 need at least 3 written quotations from approved vendors. The lowest technically acceptable quotation is selected unless the reason for choosing another vendor is documented.

## Vendor onboarding

New vendors are onboarded only after submitting GST registration, PAN, a cancelled cheque and a signed code of conduct. Vendor master data is created in SAP by the procurement team, never by the requesting department.

## Payment terms

The standard payment term is 45 days from the invoice date. MSME-registered vendors are paid within 45 days as required by law, and the finance team flags any MSME invoice older than 30 days.
