# Non-Conformance Handling Procedure

## Raising a non-conformance

Any material or product that fails inspection is tagged with a red HOLD tag and moved to the quarantine area within 1 hour. The inspector raises a non-conformance report (NCR) in the quality system on the same shift.

## Review

The material review board (MRB), made up of quality, production and engineering, reviews every NCR within 48 hours. The MRB decides one disposition: use as is, rework, return to supplier, or scrap.

## Root cause and corrective action

NCRs caused by a supplier trigger a supplier corrective action request (SCAR). The supplier must reply with a root cause analysis within 7 days and a corrective action plan within 15 days.

Internal NCRs that repeat three times in a quarter require an 8D investigation led by the quality manager.

## Customer complaints

Customer complaints are acknowledged within 24 hours and a containment action is shared with the customer within 72 hours.
