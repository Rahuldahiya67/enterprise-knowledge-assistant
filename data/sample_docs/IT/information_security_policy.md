# Information Security Policy

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and contain upper-case letters, lower-case letters, numbers and a symbol. Passwords expire every 90 days and the last 5 passwords cannot be reused.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the VPN, SAP and the HR portal.

## Laptops and devices

Company laptops use full-disk encryption and must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Personal USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops.

## Reporting incidents

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT service desk within 2 hours by calling extension 4357 or emailing it.security@northwind.example. Suspected phishing emails are reported with the "Report Phish" button in Outlook and must not be forwarded to colleagues.

## Access reviews

Access to SAP and shared drives is reviewed every quarter by the data owner. Access for employees who leave the company is revoked on their last working day.

## Data classification

Company data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer pricing and salary data are Restricted and may not be shared outside the company without approval from the data owner.
