# IT Asset Request Guide

## Requesting a laptop or monitor

New laptops, monitors and accessories are requested through the IT service portal under "Hardware Request". The request needs approval from the reporting manager.

Standard laptops are delivered within 5 working days of approval. Non-standard configurations, such as workstations for engineering design, take up to 15 working days.

## Laptop refresh cycle

Laptops are replaced every 4 years. Employees may request an early replacement if the laptop fails a hardware diagnostic run by the IT team.

## Software requests

Software not on the approved list needs a software request with a business justification. Licensed software is approved by the IT manager, and requests above Rs 25,000 per year also need finance approval.

## Returning assets

Employees leaving the company return all IT assets to the IT desk on their last working day. Lost assets are recovered from the full and final settlement at the depreciated value.
